===============================================================================
OFFICIAL EXAMINATION RULES AND ACADEMIC REGULATIONS - P5 STUDENT HANDBOOK
===============================================================================

SECTION 1: REPORTING TIME AND ENTRY REGULATIONS
Rule 1.1 - Reporting Time: Candidates must report to their assigned examination hall at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time (e.g., 8:30 AM for a 9:00 AM exam).
Rule 1.2 - Late Entry: Candidates arriving up to 15 minutes after the examination start time may be admitted at the sole discretion of the Chief Invigilator. No candidate will be admitted after 15 minutes from the start time under any circumstances.
Rule 1.3 - Early Departure: No student is permitted to leave the examination room during the first 30 minutes or the last 15 minutes of the examination session.

SECTION 2: IDENTIFICATION AND HALL TICKET REQUIREMENTS
Rule 2.1 - Identity Verification: All students must present a physical, non-expired University Student ID Card and an official Hall Ticket at the entrance of the examination hall.
Rule 2.2 - Attendance Eligibility: Students with less than 75% overall course attendance are declared ineligible to sit for final semester examinations unless granted an official medical exemption by the Academic Dean.

SECTION 3: AUTHORIZED MATERIALS AND CALCULATOR POLICY
Rule 3.1 - Electronic Devices: All mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth headphones, laptops, and smart electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall. All such devices must be switched off and placed in designated bag storage areas before entering.
Rule 3.2 - Calculator Usage: Only non-programmable, non-graphing scientific calculators are permitted for quantitative subjects (e.g., MATH301, CS202, PHY101). Programmable calculators, calculators with text memory, or smart device apps are strictly banned.
Rule 3.3 - Open-Book Policy: Examinations are strictly closed-book unless explicit written authorization for open-book reference material is provided on the exam paper header by the course instructor.

SECTION 4: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY AND MALPRACTICE
Rule 4.1 - Unfair Means: Any candidate caught copying, communicating, possessing unauthorized notes, or attempting malpractice will face immediate confiscation of their paper, expulsion from the exam, and referral to the Academic Disciplinary Committee.
Rule 4.2 - Rough Work: All rough work must be completed on the designated pages within the official answer booklet provided by the invigilator.
