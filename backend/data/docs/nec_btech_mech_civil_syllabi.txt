NARASARAOPETA ENGINEERING COLLEGE (AUTONOMOUS) - B.TECH MECHANICAL & CIVIL SYLLABUS
===============================================================================
Source URL: https://www.nrtec.in/syllabus-2/
Regulation: R20 / R23 Autonomous Curriculum
Branches: Mechanical Engineering (MECH), Civil Engineering (CIVIL)

COURSE 1: ME201 - THERMODYNAMICS & FLUID MECHANICS
- Unit 1: First & Second Laws of Thermodynamics, Concept of Entropy, Ideal Gas Laws, Carnot Cycle.
- Unit 2: IC Engines & Power Cycles: Otto, Diesel, Dual Cycles, Air Standard Efficiency.
- Unit 3: Fluid Statics & Kinematics: Pascal's Law, Continuity Equation, Bernoulli's Theorem & Applications (Venturimeter, Orifice Meter).
- Unit 4: Fluid Dynamics & Hydraulic Machines: Laminar & Turbulent Flow, Pipe Losses, Pelton Wheel, Francis Turbine, Centrifugal Pumps.
- Unit 5: Heat Transfer: Conduction (Fourier's Law), Convection (Newton's Law of Cooling), Radiation (Stefan-Boltzmann Law), Heat Exchangers.

COURSE 2: CE201 - STRENGTH OF MATERIALS & STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS
- Unit 1: Stress & Strain, Hooke's Law, Elastic Constants, Axial Loading, Thermal Stresses.
- Unit 2: Shear Force & Bending Moment Diagrams (SFD & BMD) for Simply Supported, Cantilever, & Overhanging Beams.
- Unit 3: Torsion of Circular Shafts, Power Transmission, Torsional Rigidity, Deflection of Beams (Macaulay's Method).
- Unit 4: Structural Engineering: Truss Analysis (Method of Joints, Method of Sections), Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Design.
- Unit 5: Surveying & Transportation Engineering: Chain & Compass Surveying, Levelling, Highway Geometric Design, Soil Mechanics.
